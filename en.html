ABOUT US

Hokkaido Powder Club was founded by Emi, based year-round in Asahikawa, dedicated to Asahikawa and Hokkaido's major ski resorts. Built on a decade of ski school experience, we bring you the best winter experience — lodging & transport, board storage & maintenance, ski trips, and professional training camps.

Our core belief is simple: skiing shouldn't come with the hassle of language, transport, and gear logistics. From the moment you step off the plane to the moment you head home after the powder, every step should be taken care of — that's what a real ski getaway looks like.
