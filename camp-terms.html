一、連續轉彎保證1. The Linked-Turns Guarantee

達成標準What counts as success
雙板＝在教練指定的初級道，獨立連續轉彎滑完全程；單板＝同一條件下連續換刃。Day 4 最後一堂課由教練驗收並錄影，影片為判定依據。trick 不在保證範圍內。Ski: ride the green run chosen by your instructor from top to bottom on your own, linking turns. Snowboard: the same, linking edge changes. Your instructor checks and films this in the last lesson of Day 4; the video decides. Tricks are not covered.
適用條件（全部符合才適用）Conditions (all must apply)
10 歲以上；出席 Day 2–4 全部課程；遵守教練指示；出發前看完 3 支暖身影片（以系統觀看紀錄為準）。Age 10 or older; attend every lesson on Days 2–4; follow your instructor; watch all 3 warm-up videos before arrival (as recorded by our system).
不適用情形Not covered
缺課、遲到超過 1 小時、飲酒後上課；受傷或身體不適無法上課；不可抗力導致課程縮短且未補課。Missed lessons, arriving more than 1 hour late, or skiing after drinking; being unable to take part due to injury or illness; lessons shortened by force majeure and not made up.
重上內容What you get
下一季（雪季結束前）免費 3 天課程，每天 5 小時、師生比最多 1:3，跟當季其他班次併班。住宿、交通、雪票、租借依當季價格另計。3 free lesson days next season (before the season ends), 5 hours a day, max 1:3, joined to an existing camp. Lodging, transport, lift pass and rental are charged at that season's prices.
重上限制Limits
限本人、限一次、不可轉讓、不可換現金；依當季剩餘名額安排，旺季不適用；需在下一季開放報名後 60 天內預約。For you only, once, non-transferable and not exchangeable for cash; subject to availability and not valid in peak season; must be booked within 60 days of next season's bookings opening.
申請方式How to claim
Day 4 驗收未達成時，教練當場登記並發出重上證明（Email），憑證明預約。If you don't pass the Day 4 check, your instructor registers it on the spot and emails you a retake certificate. Use it to book.

二、付款2. Payment

  • 付訂金即保留名額（每人金額見報價信）。Your deposit holds your seats (amount per person in your quote).
  • 尾款於出發前 30 天付清。未如期付清，視同取消。The balance is due 30 days before arrival. Unpaid balances count as a cancellation.
  • 價格依報名批次而定，付訂金時的價格為最終價格。Price depends on the release you book in and is locked when you pay the deposit.

三、開班3. Camp minimums

  • 每班最多 3 人。2 人報名即保證開班，不改期、不取消。Max 3 people per camp. With 2 people the camp is guaranteed to run.
  • 開班前 30 天仍未達 2 人時，可以改期或全額退費。If a camp has fewer than 2 people 30 days before it starts, you can move dates or get a full refund.

四、取消與退款4. Cancellations and refunds

不論任何原因，依取消日計算：Whatever the reason, refunds depend on when you cancel:

出發前 31 天以上31+ days before退還已付金額 50%50% of the amount paid
出發前 8–30 天8–30 days before退還已付金額 30%30% of the amount paid
出發前 7 天內／未到Within 7 days / no-show不退款No refund
  • 退款金額會扣除 Stripe 手續費。Refunds are less Stripe fees.
  • 天候或雪場停駛時，我們會改到旭川周邊其他雪場上課，不另退款。If weather closes the resort, we move lessons to another ski area around Asahikawa; this is not grounds for a refund.
  • 請務必購買涵蓋滑雪／單板的旅平險。Travel insurance covering skiing/snowboarding is required.
