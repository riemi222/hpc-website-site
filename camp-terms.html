一、方案與人數1. The plan and class size

  • 每班 1 位教練，1–6 人，1 人即開班。Each class has one instructor and 1–6 students. Classes run with just 1.
  • 每人價格依同一筆報名的人數計算（見價目表）；同一班的不同組別，各自照自己那一組的人數計價。The per-person price depends on how many are in your booking (see the price table). Different groups in the same class each pay by their own group size.
  • 僅限 10 歲以上（含）。Ages 10 and up.

二、付款2. Payment

  • 付訂金即確認報名（每人 ¥50,000，金額見報價信）。Paying the deposit confirms your booking (¥50,000 per person, shown in your quote).
  • 尾款於出發前 10 天付清，出發前 30 天會用 Email 寄送尾款付款連結。未如期付清，視同取消。出發前 10 天內報名，需一次付清全額。The balance is due 10 days before arrival; we email you a payment link 30 days before. Unpaid balances count as a cancellation. If you book within 10 days of arrival, you pay in full.

三、人數變動3. Group size changes

  • 加人需同一班還有名額。同一筆報名的人數有變動時（加人或有人取消），每人價格依最後的人數重新計算。加人時，新加入的人另付訂金，大家的價格跟著調降；有人取消時，取消的人依取消規定退款，其餘的人依最後人數調整價格，差額併入尾款。已付清尾款後才調降的，退還差額。Adding someone requires a free seat in your class. If your group size changes (someone joins or cancels), per-person prices are recalculated using the final group size. When someone joins, they pay a deposit and everyone's price drops. When someone cancels, their refund follows the cancellation terms and the rest of the group's price is adjusted to the final size, with the difference added to the balance. If the balance has already been paid, any reduction is refunded.

四、機場接送（加購）4. Airport transfer (add-on)

  • 新千歲機場⇄旭川住處，一台車單程：1–3 人 ¥85,000、4–6 人 ¥90,000。夜間航班（22:00–05:00）每趟加收 ¥20,000，由我們另外確認。New Chitose Airport ⇄ your Asahikawa apartment, per vehicle one way: ¥85,000 for 1–3 people, ¥90,000 for 4–6. Night flights (22:00–05:00) are +¥20,000 per leg, confirmed by us separately.
  • Day 1 請在 14:00 前抵達旭川住處集合。加購接機的話，班機請在 10:30 前降落新千歲機場（車程約 3 小時）。Please arrive at your Asahikawa apartment by 14:00 on Day 1. If you add the airport pickup, your flight should land at New Chitose by 10:30 (the drive takes about 3 hours).

  • 天候或雪場停駛時，我們會改到旭川周邊其他雪場上課，不另退款。If weather closes the resort, we move lessons to another ski area around Asahikawa; this is not grounds for a refund.
