為什麼The problem

第一次滑雪，為什麼大部分人都帶著瘀青回家？Why most first-timers go home sore, not skiing

報了十幾人的大班課，教練顧不到你，一整天都在跌倒。You join a group lesson of 12. The instructor barely sees you. You spend the day on your backside.

FAQ

常見問題Questions

完全沒滑過可以報嗎？I've never skied. Can I join?

可以，這個班就是為完全新手設計的。Yes. This camp is built for complete beginners.

只有我一個人可以報嗎？Can I sign up alone?

可以，我們會把你和其他同程度的學員編成一班。開班前 30 天仍未達 2 人時，可以改期或全額退費。Yes. We'll group you with other beginners at your level. If your camp still has fewer than 2 people 30 days before it starts, you can move dates or get a full refund.

不足 3 人會開班嗎？Does the camp run with fewer than 3?

會。只要 2 人報名就保證開班，不改期、不取消，課程內容和保證條款完全一樣。Yes. With 2 people the camp is guaranteed to run — no date changes, no cancellations, same lessons and same guarantee.

小孩可以參加嗎？Can children join?

這個班僅限 10 歲以上（含）參加。This camp is for ages 10 and up.

可以自己帶雪具嗎？Can I bring my own gear?

可以，但價格不變。Yes, but the price stays the same.

下雪太大、雪場停駛怎麼辦？What if a storm closes the resort?

我們會改到旭川周邊其他雪場上課。We move your lesson to another ski area around Asahikawa.

怎麼付款？How do I pay?

報價信裡有訂金付款連結（每人 ¥，信用卡），付了就保留名額。尾款於出發前 10 天付清（出發前 10 天內報名需一次付清）。Your quote email has a deposit link (¥ per person, card). Paying holds your seats. The balance is due 10 days before arrival (book within 10 days and you pay in full).
