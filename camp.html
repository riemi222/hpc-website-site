FAQ

常見問題Questions

完全沒滑過可以報嗎？I've never skied. Can I join?

可以，這個方案就是為完全新手設計的。教練會從怎麼穿雪靴開始教。Yes. It's built for complete beginners. Your instructor starts from putting on your boots.

一個人可以報嗎？Can I book on my own?

可以，1 人就開班，付訂金就確認，不用等湊人。1 人報名每人 ¥（淡季）；揪朋友一起，每人會便宜很多。Yes. We run with just 1, and you're confirmed as soon as you pay the deposit. No waiting for others. Booking alone is ¥ (low season); bring friends and everyone pays much less.

價格怎麼依人數計算？How does group size affect the price?

依同一筆報名的人數計算每人價格，人越多每人越便宜，例如淡季 3 人每人 ¥、6 人每人 ¥。如果同一班有不同組的人，每一組都照自己那一組的人數計價。完整價目表在上方。The per-person price depends on how many are in your booking: in low season, ¥ each for 3 and ¥ each for 6. If different groups share a class, each group pays by its own size. See the full table above.

一班最多幾人？How many per class?

每班 1 位教練、合計最多 6 人。7 人以上請傳訊息給我們，我們會安排同日期兩班並另外報價。One instructor and up to 6 people in total. 7 or more? Message us and we'll set up two classes on the same dates with a custom quote.

報名後人數可以變動嗎？Can our group size change after booking?

可以。加人要同一班還有名額，請傳訊息給我們。同一筆報名的人數有變動時（加人或有人取消），每人價格依最後的人數重新計算。加人時，新加入的人另付訂金，大家的價格跟著調降；有人取消時，取消的人依取消規定退款，其餘的人依最後人數調整價格，差額併入尾款。已付清尾款後才調降的，退還差額。Yes. To add someone, your class needs a free seat; just message us. If your group size changes (someone joins or cancels), per-person prices are recalculated using the final group size. When someone joins, they pay a deposit and everyone's price drops. When someone cancels, their refund follows the cancellation terms and the rest of the group's price is adjusted to the final size, with the difference added to the balance. If the balance has already been paid, any reduction is refunded.
